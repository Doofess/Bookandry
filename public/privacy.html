Privacy Policy

Effective 7 October 2026

Bookandry (bookandry.com) is a household money tracker: income, expenses and every currency, for everyone in your household. It is run by Rotem Grinhut ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the service collects, why, where it is kept, and what you can do about it. Questions: Rotem2207@gmail.com.

What is collected

  • Account details. Your email address. If you sign in with Google: the name, email address and profile picture link Google shares with the app. Your password is never stored, only a one-way hash of it. A passkey is stored as its public key only. If you turn on two-step sign-in, the authenticator secret is stored so your codes can be checked.
  • What you enter. The names of the people in your household, income and expenses, categories, payment methods, recurring commitments, notes, currencies and settings.
  • Linked accounts. If you invite someone to share your household: the email address you invite, until the invitation is accepted, declined, cancelled or expires (seven days), and which accounts are linked together, with the name and picture each account shows the others.
  • Technical data. IP address, browser type and request times, in the hosting providers' logs. Used only to run and protect the service.

How it is used

Only to provide the service: to sign you in, to keep your data safe and the same on every device you sign in on, and to protect the service from abuse. There is no advertising, no analytics or tracking, and no profiling. Your data is never sold, and never shared for marketing.

Where it is kept

  • On your device: a full copy in your browser's storage, so the app is fast and works offline. Signing out removes it.
  • On the server: in a database run by Supabase in Seoul, South Korea. Your records are stored encrypted, under a key your account holds. Because the service holds that key, this is not end-to-end encryption: the operator could technically read the data. I do not, except where you ask me to or the law requires it.

Linked accounts

When accounts are linked, every linked account can see and edit the household's data: everyone's income and expenses, cards, bank accounts, categories and the names the household uses - and sees each linked account's name and picture. Nothing else of your account is shared: not your email address, password, passkeys, two-step sign-in or settings. Anyone can unlink at any time; each account then keeps only its own entries, cards and accounts, and stops seeing the others'.

Who else processes it

  • Supabase, Inc.: the database and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare, Inc.: hosting and delivering the website.
  • Google LLC: only if you choose to sign in with Google.
  • Resend, Inc.: sending the account's emails, such as confirming an address, resetting a password, or an invitation you send to link accounts (the invited address, your name and email address, and the invitation's link).
  • Anthropic, PBC, for two things, both only while you are signed in:
    • Choosing a category for you: what you type as the name of a transaction is sent, with the names of your categories, so Claude can pick the one it belongs in. Not the amount, the date or anything about you.
    • "Smart import": the files you choose or share to the app - screenshots, photos, PDFs, spreadsheets, documents - are sent to be read, with the names of your categories and payment methods - and, in a linked household, the names of its people, so the file's holder can be matched to one of them. They can include amounts, dates, shops and names. Before anything is sent, spreadsheets are read on your device and only their first rows go; a PDF goes as its text when it has one. Files pass through our server (Supabase Edge Functions) and are not stored by Bookandry. Of a card number, only the last four digits come back; a bank account's number comes back only for an account you then choose to add. Anthropic does not keep this content by default, beyond what its safety systems flag, and does not train on it. Reading is limited to a number of times a day per person. A file is read each time you add it, and what was read is not kept.
  • Exchange-rate services (Frankfurter at api.frankfurter.dev, and ExchangeRate-API at open.er-api.com): when an amount is converted, your browser asks them for the rate. They receive the currencies and date asked for, and your IP address. Never your transactions.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours, under their own security and privacy commitments.

Google user data

Information received from Google is used only to sign you in and to show which account you are signed in with. It is not used for anything else and not shared with anyone. Bookandry's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Cookies and local storage

No cookies are used for tracking. Your browser's local storage holds your sign-in session, your settings and your copy of the data: what the app needs in order to work.

How long it is kept

For as long as your account exists. Deleting your account (Settings → Delete account) deletes its data from the server and from that device. Other devices that were signed in show a sign-in screen, from which their copy can be removed. Backups and logs at the providers may keep copies for a limited time before they are overwritten.

You can delete all of your data from Settings › Data & backup. When your account is linked with others, everyone else in the household must confirm by email first, and then the data is deleted for every account in it.

Your rights

You can see, export (Settings → Export a backup), correct and delete your data yourself at any time. You can also email me to ask for a copy of your data, a correction, or deletion; I will answer within 30 days. You may complain to a data-protection authority; in Israel, that is the Privacy Protection Authority.

Security

The connection is always encrypted (HTTPS), records are stored encrypted, the site runs under a strict content security policy, and passkeys and two-step sign-in are available. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, you will be told.

Children

Bookandry is not intended for children under 16.

Changes

A new version of this policy will be posted here with a new date. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
